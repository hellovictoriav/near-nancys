After the down payment · monthly carrying costs

What the house costs to keep.

Savings get us to closing. This page is the quieter question: once we own it, what do four family units need to set aside every month — taxes, insurance, pool, beach, utilities, and a maintenance reserve — so nobody is surprised in February.

These are planning ranges for Martha's Vineyard homes in our search band (prefer under $2M, Oak Bluffs / Tisbury / Edgartown / West Tisbury). They are not quotes, not lender figures, and not a broker pitch. Assessment is not the same as sale price — Island assessments often lag the market.

Property taxes FY2026 residential mil rates

Towns tax assessed value, not list price. Rate is dollars per $1,000 of assessment. Most Island towns also add a Community Preservation Act (CPA) surcharge — Tea Lane's rule of thumb is about 3% of the tax on assessed value after the first $100,000.

$4.99Oak Bluffs / $1,000
$7.42Tisbury (VH) / $1,000
$2.48Edgartown / $1,000
$5.21West Tisbury / $1,000

Sources: Tea Lane Associates FY2026 mil-rate sheet; Oak Bluffs / West Tisbury / Edgartown confirmations echoed by Austin RE (Feb 2026) and town hearing coverage. Tisbury residential exemption is 22% for qualifying year-round owners; Oak Bluffs 15%; West Tisbury 30%. Edgartown uses a single rate. A seasonal family house usually does not get the residential exemption — plan without it.

Edgartown's mil rate is the lowest on the Island; Tisbury's is the highest among our four towns. A $1.7M assessment in Edgartown can cost less in tax than a $1.4M assessment in Tisbury. Always pull the actual assessor card before you fall in love with a number.

Pool impact taxes + operating
On the tax bill

Assessed value bump

Public parcel data rarely isolates “pool only” on Vineyard cards. Planning range used here: an in-ground pool often adds roughly $25,000–$80,000 to assessed value (sometimes more for new gunite + patio). At Oak Bluffs $4.99 / $1,000, that is about $125–$400/yr of extra tax; at Tisbury $7.42, about $185–$595/yr.

Thin public comps — treat as a planning band, not an assessor quote. Confirm on the specific parcel.

To keep the water open

Operating costs

Separate from taxes: opening / closing, chemicals, filter & pump electricity, cover, and a modest insurance bump. Cape & Islands seasonal pools commonly land around $2,500–$5,500/yr all-in for a family-size in-ground (~$210–$460/mo equivalent). Heated or larger can run higher.

Calculator default mid-range: $3,800/yr (~$317/mo).

HOA & association fees many Island houses pay $0

Most single-family Vineyard homes have no HOA. Condo, co-op, and some small subdivisions do. When they exist, annual dues on the Island often fall in a wide band — roughly $1,200–$6,000/yr for lighter condo/association setups, and higher for full-service buildings. Always read the condo docs; dues can jump for roofs and roads.

Private beach / neighborhood associations (East Chop–style clubs, Harthaven-style beaches, Sengekontacket-type waterfront associations) are different from a town beach sticker. Dues are often opaque until you ask the listing broker. Planning range used here for a private beach association: $500–$3,000/yr per household, sometimes plus a boat or tennis facility fee. East Chop Yacht Club facility fees (for boats) are published separately and are not the same as a house HOA.

Data gap: Harthaven / East Chop residential association dues are not published the way town tax rates are. Confirm in writing before offer.

Beach access fees town stickers vs private

Down-Island public beaches (Oak Bluffs Inkwell / Town Beach, Joseph Sylvia State Beach, Edgartown South Beach / Katama, Vineyard Haven Owen Park) are generally free to use — no town sticker for the sand itself. Parking can still be scarce in July.

West Tisbury — Lambert's Cove (2026 town schedule): car sticker $75 for qualifying residents or renters with a lease; walk-on $15; year-round resident pass $125 (voter list + cars registered in town). Stickers required roughly mid-June through Labor Day, 7 a.m.–5 p.m. After 5 p.m. and in the off-season the beach opens more broadly. Source: West Tisbury Parks & Recreation.

Up-Island resident beaches (Chilmark Lucy Vincent / Squibnocket) require town stickers; those towns are outside our primary search, but they matter if friends invite you.

Farms End / Lambert's Cove is our taste reference for what summer beach access has felt like as renters — not a claim about exact sticker amounts we paid in past seasons, and not a promise that ownership automatically unlocks every beach.

Homeowners insurance Cape & Islands is expensive

Dukes County sits at the high end of Massachusetts homeowners insurance. County-level escrow medians recently around ~$4,000/yr (HearthMap / NBER escrow data through 2024) understate what a $1.5–2M coastal or near-coastal house actually quotes — ZIP-level FIO samples for Vineyard Haven and Edgartown have run much higher, and 2024–2025 brought nonrenewals and steep renewals Island-wide (WBUR, MV Times).

Planning range for a primary or seasonal MV home in our price band: $5,000–$14,000/yr (~$420–$1,170/mo), before a separate flood policy if the parcel is in a flood zone. Wind / named-storm deductibles are common. FAIR Plan exists as a last resort but has coverage caps that may not rebuild a Vineyard house at today's costs.

Calculator default: $8,500/yr. Get real quotes early — insurance can kill a deal that taxes alone would not.

Utilities & caretaker seasonal vs year-round
Mostly summer use

Electric, propane or oil for shoulder heat, town water/sewer or well + Title 5 septic pump-out, trash, internet. Winterization + a local caretaker / check-ins matter if the house sits empty. Planning: $4,500–$9,000/yr (~$375–$750/mo), plus caretaker if needed (~$150–$400/mo in the off-season is a common family band — confirm locally).

Year-round heat

Same stack, but heat and electric climb. Oil / propane Island delivery is not cheap. Planning: $8,000–$16,000/yr (~$670–$1,330/mo) for a 5-bed-ish house, highly dependent on insulation, heat type, and how many months someone is home.

Calculator lets you pick seasonal or year-round midpoints.

Maintenance reserve Island houses weather hard

Rule of thumb: about 1% of home value per year, or roughly $3–$6 per finished square foot, for an Island house — roofs, paint, cedar, appliances, landscaping, driveway, and septic surprises. On a $1.7M house that is ~$17,000/yr (~$1,417/mo) as a reserve target, not a mandatory bill. Years will be quieter; some years will not.

Calculator uses 1% of the value you enter. Lower it if the house is new; raise it if the listing says “sold as-is.”
