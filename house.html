How we share the house · kind and specific

Summer is prime. Guests are welcome.

This is a family house, not a fortress and not a hotel. The notes below are so nobody has to guess. They are not a lease.

The middle two weeks of August

Those two weeks are all-together weeks. Fewer extra guests then, so Jannah, Kenya, Jacqueline, Kiyanna, Edna, and Victoria can actually be in the same rooms. Friends can still come if the family invites them as a group — just not a stack of separate plus-ones that turns the house into a boarding house.

Mid-August is not first-come. It is family time we protect on purpose.

How to request space propose, name people, wait for a yes
  1. 1

    Propose dates early

    The earlier, the kinder. A March note about July is a gift. A Thursday text about Saturday is a squeeze.

  2. 2

    Say who is coming

    Names, not just a headcount. "Me plus two" is hard to plan beds around. "Kenya, Maya, and Uncle Ray" is something we can say yes to.

  3. 3

    Wait for a clear yes

    Silence is not a yes. A thumbs-up in a group chat is closer. If you haven't heard back, ask once more — don't assume the house is empty.

When two units want the same week especially prime summer

Write one request

One written ask — here, or in the family thread — with dates and names. Give everyone 48 hours to object or offer a split. After that, treat a quiet "ok" as a yes.

Split it, or take turns

If two units want the same prime week, try splitting the week first. If that doesn't work, take turns year to year. Nobody "wins" August by asking first.

Disagreements stay small: one request, 48 hours, then a conversation. The house is supposed to make us closer, not keep score.
